# Protama

## Pronunciation

*proh-TAH-mah*

## Part of speech

**Protama** is a proper noun and coined technical term. It is the name of a
synthetic-intelligence architecture and the research project developing it.

## Definition

Protama refers to a developmental structural-learning system designed to
acquire, refine, and apply latent relational structure from limited examples.
The name identifies a specific architecture and body of research, in the same
way that names such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, and AlphaZero identify specific
technical systems.

## Official project interpretation

Protama is an intentionally coined name. Its official project interpretation
combines:

- **pro-:** forward, ahead, or toward;
- **-tama:** the structural core or essential pattern that a system seeks to
  discover and work with.

The name therefore carries the intended meaning:

> **A system moving toward structural essence.**

The shorter interpretations **“toward structure”** and **“forward pattern”**
are also consistent with the name.

The `-tama` interpretation is a project-defined association within a coined
technical name. Protama is not being presented as a direct historical
derivation from another language.

## Usage

- “Protama inferred a new relational hypothesis.”
- “The Protama system rejected the near-miss because of a type violation.”
- “The evaluation measures whether Protama transfers structure to unfamiliar
  examples.”
- “A CPU Protama can provide a lower-power deployment.”
- “A GPU Protama can support higher-throughput workloads.”

## Category

Protama is:

- a proper noun;
- a coined technical term;
- a synthetic-intelligence architecture;
- a machine-learning and cognitive-systems research project;

## Naming principle

The name reflects the project's central direction: progress toward systems
that learn relationships and structural patterns rather than relying only on
surface resemblance or memorized outputs.
