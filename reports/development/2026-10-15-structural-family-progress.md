# Structural Family Memory: Public Development Update

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Status:** development update

## Summary

Protama development is progressing from controlled structured-memory
experiments toward the broader Structural Induction and Transfer Gate.

The current public result is a research milestone, not a claim that the full
benchmark has passed.

## Publicly reportable progress

The private development work has produced progress in:

- representing reusable relationships in controlled experiments;
- testing transfer to unfamiliar structured examples;
- distinguishing valid examples from selected near-misses;
- making insufficient or ambiguous evidence visible to evaluation;
- strengthening reproducibility and evaluation boundaries.

The exact implementation, internal data structures, and matching behavior are
not part of this public update.

## Remaining public gates

The following capabilities remain to be demonstrated publicly:

- reliable structure extraction from raw input;
- transfer across controlled changes in wording and vocabulary;
- robust near-miss discrimination across benchmark categories;
- trace-grounded explanations;
- reproducible scoring and independent result verification;
- a complete passing result for the Structural Induction and Transfer Gate.

## Interpretation

The accurate public claim is:

> Protama is developing and testing a structural-learning capability under
> controlled evaluation conditions.

The repository does not claim:

> Protama has solved independent natural-language structural invention.

Future reports will publish only the information needed to reproduce and
evaluate a released artifact. Private implementation details and trade-secret
know-how will remain outside the public repository.
