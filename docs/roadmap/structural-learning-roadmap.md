# Structural Learning Roadmap

This roadmap communicates public research milestones without exposing the
private implementation plan.

## Current public direction

Protama is being developed toward a system that can learn reusable structure,
transfer it to unfamiliar examples, reject typed near-misses, abstain under
uncertainty, and retain evidence for later review.

## Public milestones

### Structured-memory foundation

Establish a controlled foundation for testing reusable relationships on
structured observations. This milestone is narrower than natural-language
understanding and will be reported as such.

### Evidence and decision auditability

Make evaluation outcomes inspectable enough to distinguish support,
contradiction, and insufficient evidence. Public reports will describe the
observable contract without publishing internal record layouts.

### Order and ambiguity robustness

Ensure that equivalent structured inputs are not judged differently merely
because their presentation order changes, and ensure that ambiguous cases are
not resolved by unsupported guessing.

### Raw-input structural learning

Connect raw input processing to structural evaluation only after the
structured foundation is stable and independently measurable.

### Benchmark integration

Release reproducible generators, scoring tools, and evaluation reports only
after their public boundary, licensing, and leakage controls have been
reviewed.

## What the roadmap does not claim

The roadmap is not a claim that Protama has achieved general intelligence,
independent natural-language structural invention, or a passing benchmark
result. Each capability must be demonstrated with a versioned evaluation and
clearly stated input conditions.

Internal phase names, algorithms, schemas, and integration details remain
private.
